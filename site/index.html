Features

Everything you need to record attendance

Event area check

Your GPS position is compared with the venue. Check-in opens only when you are inside the event area.

Fingerprint or face

Confirm it is you with the biometrics already on your phone. PEAM never stores your fingerprint or face.

No signal? No problem

Attendance is saved on your phone first and sent automatically once you are back online.

Hard to fake

Blocks fake GPS apps, ignores a changed phone clock, and checks the app with Google Play Integrity.

Timely reminders

Get notified about new events, 30 minutes before they start, and when you still need to check out.

Your attendance history

See every check-in and check-out, and whether PHRMO already has it or it is still waiting to send.

Install

Get the app in a minute

  1. Scan the QR code or tap Download for Android.
  2. Open the downloaded peam-release.apk.
  3. If asked, allow your browser to install unknown apps.
  4. Open PEAM and sign in with your Employee ID.

Requires Android 7.0 or newer with location and a screen lock (fingerprint or face) set up. The first phone you sign in with becomes your registered phone. To switch phones, request it in the app and PHRMO will approve it.

Built with privacy in mind

  • Fingerprint and face checks happen inside your phone. Only "yes" or "no" reaches the app.
  • Location is read only while you check in or check out, never in the background.
  • You can see only your own records. Only PHRMO staff can review attendance.
  • Every attendance is checked again by the server, and doubtful records go to PHRMO, not to an automated decision.
